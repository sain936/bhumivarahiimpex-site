Dried Red Chilli

Guntur Teja, 334 Sannam and Byadgi chillies, whole, crushed or powder.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

Andhra Pradesh is the heart of Indian chilli, and Guntur is the largest chilli market in Asia. We source the main export varieties for heat, colour or both.

Teja is chosen for strong heat, 334 Sannam for balanced heat and colour, and Byadgi for deep red colour with mild heat.

Available types

Teja (S17)

High heat, bright red. Popular in China, Southeast Asia and the Middle East.

334 / S4 Sannam

Medium heat and good colour. The most traded Guntur variety.

Byadgi and Wonder Hot

Deep colour and mild heat, for paprika-style uses and colour extraction.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order for dried red chilli?

One 40 ft high-cube container (about 12 – 14 MT) is most economical, as chilli is light. 20 ft loads are possible.

Which chilli should I choose?

Choose Teja for strong heat, 334 Sannam for a balance of heat and colour, and Byadgi for colour with mild heat.

Do you provide a lab report?

Yes. Each lot can be shipped with a lab analysis report, and we can arrange testing to your market’s limits (for example EU pesticide residue limits) on request.

Can I get a sample before ordering?

Yes. We can courier a sample of the exact lot for your approval before the contract is confirmed.
