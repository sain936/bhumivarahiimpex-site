## Rules to follow on every platform

### What not to claim yet

- **Do not tick certifications you don't hold yet.** IEC, APEDA RCMC, Spices Board registration, FSSAI, ISO and organic are all fields on these platforms. Leave them blank or write "in progress" until the certificate is issued. Platforms and buyers check, and a false tick can get the account suspended.
- **Do not post a GST certificate, IEC or bank details as a public image.** Most platforms ask for them only for verification, which stays private.
- **Do not claim to own mills or warehouses, and do not claim years of experience or export volumes.** Say "sourced from established mills", as the website does.
- **Use the same company name, phone and email everywhere.** They match the website and Google, which builds trust.

### Handling enquiries

- **Reply within 24 hours, ideally within 2.** IndiaMART and TradeIndia rank sellers by how often and how fast they reply.
- **Ask the same four questions every time:** product and grade, quantity, destination port, and packing. Then quote using the quotation template in the letterhead folder.
- **Log every serious lead in the RFQ Google Sheet,** even if it came from IndiaMART or WhatsApp, so it shows in the Monday weekly summary.
- **Watch for scams.** Ignore buyers who ask for free samples to be couriered to random addresses, who offer to "pay extra" through unknown agents, or who ask for advance fees to "register" you. Real importers ask for specs, a proforma invoice and payment terms.
