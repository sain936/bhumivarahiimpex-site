## How to use this kit

**Who to contact:** rice and spice importers, wholesalers, distributors, supermarket buyers and private-label brands. Start with the Gulf (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait), then West and East Africa, then Europe.

**Where to find them:**

- **Buy leads** on IndiaMART, TradeIndia, ExportersIndia and Go4WorldBusiness. Answer each one with the reply-to-enquiry email below.
- **LinkedIn search:** for example "rice importer Dubai", "procurement manager food trading Saudi" or "importateur riz Sénégal". Connect first, then send the WhatsApp or LinkedIn version.
- **Trade directories:** the Dubai Chamber member directory, Yellow Pages UAE and Saudi, and Kompass. Once you are registered, add the lists from APEDA and Spices Board buyer-seller meets.
- **Trade fairs:** Gulfood (Dubai), SIAL (Paris), Anuga (Cologne) and the APEDA India pavilions. The exhibitor lists are public, so you can use them even without a stand.

**Which language to use:**

- **English** for the Gulf, India-linked buyers, East Africa and Europe.
- **Arabic** for Saudi Arabia, Oman, Yemen, Egypt, Iraq, Jordan and North Africa. Many Gulf buyers also read English, so send the Arabic with the English underneath.
- **French** for Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Mali, Guinea, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Benin, Togo, Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia.

**Follow-up rhythm:**

| Day | What to send |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Intro email (or WhatsApp after a LinkedIn connection) |
| 4 | Follow-up 1: short, with one product and the catalogue |
| 10 | Follow-up 2: a polite last note |
| Any reply | Answer within 24 hours, then send a quotation on the letterhead template |

Send from **info@bhumivarahiimpex.com** with the branded Gmail signature, so that SPF, DKIM and DMARC protect delivery. From a new domain, send no more than 30–40 new cold emails a day, or Gmail may flag the account.

## WhatsApp and LinkedIn messages (English)

## Rules

### Keep the domain healthy

- **Write to one person at a time** from Gmail, using their name and company. Don't use bulk tools or BCC lists. Gmail and buyers' servers treat mass sends from a new domain as spam, and that would also hurt the RFQ acknowledgement emails.
- **Send at most 30–40 new cold emails a day** for the first two months.
- **Stop after Follow-up 2.** If someone asks not to be contacted, remove them at once and note it in the sheet.
- **For EU and UK contacts,** only write to business addresses found on the company's own site or a trade listing, and say how you found them if asked. That keeps the outreach within GDPR's rules for business contact.

### What not to claim

The same rules apply as in the listing kit. Don't mention IEC, APEDA, Spices Board, FSSAI, ISO or organic certification until the certificate is issued. If a buyer asks, say the registrations are in progress. Don't claim your own mills, years in business or past export volumes.

### Track every contact

Add a tab called **Outreach** to the RFQ Google Sheet with these columns:

| Date | Company | Country | Contact name | Email / WhatsApp | Product interest | Language | Stage | Next follow-up | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Stage** can be: Sent, Follow-up 1, Follow-up 2, Replied, Quoted, Sample, Order, or Not interested.

Check the **Next follow-up** column each morning. When someone asks for a price, move them to the main RFQ tab so they show in the Monday summary.

### Weekly target to start

Contact 20 new companies, answer every reply within 24 hours, and send at least 3 quotations.
