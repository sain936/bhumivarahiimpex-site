HOW WE WORK

From Inquiry to Delivery

A clear, documented five-step export process, so you always know where your order stands.

  1. 1

    Inquiry & Specs

    Share commodity, grade, quantity, packaging and destination port through our RFQ desk.

  2. 2

    Quotation

    Receive a detailed offer with pricing, Incoterms (FOB / CFR / CIF) and shipment timeline.

  3. 3

    Contract

    Confirm specifications, quantity, payment terms and delivery schedule in a written sales contract.

  4. 4

    Quality & Documents

    Pre-shipment inspection, phytosanitary and fumigation certificates, and export documentation.

    Shipment

    Container loading at Kakinada, Visakhapatnam or Chennai, with tracking until arrival.

BUYER FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which products do you export?

Rice (1010 white, parboiled, broken and 1121 basmati), Indian spices such as Nizamabad turmeric and Guntur Teja red chilli, pulses and grains, and custom industrial goods on request.

Which Incoterms do you offer?

We quote on FOB, CFR and CIF terms. Tell us your discharge port in the RFQ form and we will price accordingly.

Which ports do you ship from?

Shipments are loaded at Kakinada, Visakhapatnam and Chennai ports on the east coast of India.

How is the cargo packed?

Packaging follows buyer specification, for example 25 kg or 50 kg PP bags, with custom branding and marking on request.

How is quality verified?

Cargo is inspected before dispatch, and third-party inspection by agencies such as SGS or Bureau Veritas can be arranged along with phytosanitary and fumigation certificates.

How quickly will I get a quotation?

Our export desk responds to complete RFQs within 24 business hours.
