1010 Non-Basmati Rice

Long-grain parboiled and raw rice from Andhra Pradesh, at a dependable everyday price.

1010 is a long-grain non-basmati rice grown widely in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, close to our loading ports. It gives buyers a reliable, affordable everyday rice for wholesale, food service and government or institutional tenders.

Because the growing area sits near Kakinada and Visakhapatnam ports, we can move 1010 quickly from mill to vessel. We offer it parboiled or raw, in 5%, 25% or 100% broken grades.

Available types

1010 Parboiled

Parboiled for a firm, separate grain and longer shelf life. Popular across West and East Africa.

1010 Raw (White)

Milled and polished white rice for buyers who prefer a softer texture.

5%, 25% and 100% broken, for price-sensitive markets and food processing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order for 1010 rice?

Our standard minimum is one 20 ft container (about 26 – 27 MT). Smaller trial lots can be discussed for new buyers.

Parboiled or raw: which should I choose?

Parboiled keeps the grains firm and separate and stores longer, so it is the most popular choice in Africa. Raw white rice is softer and is often chosen for domestic retail.

Which ports do you ship from?

Mainly Kakinada and Visakhapatnam, which are closest to the 1010 growing area, as well as Chennai.

Can I get a sample before ordering?

Yes. We can courier a sample of the exact lot for your approval before the contract is confirmed.
