1509 Basmati Rice

Long, slender basmati with excellent elongation and a sharper price than 1121.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

1509 (Pusa Basmati 1509) is an early-maturing basmati variety with grain length close to 1121. It cooks long and fluffy, and it usually costs less than 1121, so it is popular with importers who want premium looks at a keener price.

It is widely traded in the Gulf, Europe and Africa in Steam and Sella forms.

Available types

1509 Steam

Steam-processed for a firm, non-sticky cooked grain with a creamy white colour.

1509 Golden Sella

Parboiled for a golden colour and strong grains that stay separate after cooking.

1509 White Sella / Raw

White Sella (parboiled, white colour) or Raw (unprocessed) for a softer texture and fresher aroma.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order for 1509 Basmati?

Our standard minimum is one 20 ft container (about 24 – 25 MT). Smaller trial lots can be discussed for new buyers.

How is 1509 different from 1121?

1509 grains are very slightly shorter but cook almost as long. It is usually priced below 1121, which makes it a good value alternative.

Can you pack under our own brand?

Yes. We can print your brand on BOPP or non-woven bags in any of the standard sizes.
